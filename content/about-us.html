About Us

Why Tasbih Counts Exists

Most dhikr counters make you work for the count. Some hide the tap target behind a small button, some put an advertisement in the middle of the tool, and several lose everything the moment you reload the page.

Tasbih Counts is built the other way round. The online tasbih counter is the first thing on the page, the whole counting area is the tap target, and nothing about pricing or signing in appears before you have counted.

How It Is Built

  • No account to count. The counter, the dhikr library, guided routines, the 99 Names, your own dhikr, streaks and stats are free for everyone, with no sign-up.
  • Offline first. After the first visit it works with no connection at all, and it installs to your home screen.
  • Your dhikr stays yours. Without an account, nothing you count leaves your device. We never sell your data or use your practice for advertising.
  • Calm on purpose. No streak guilt, no confetti and no notifications chasing you back — unless you ask for a daily reminder.

The Religious Content

Every phrase, transliteration, meaning and count is kept under version control and reviewed. Nothing is machine translated, and no phrase is published on the strength of a search result.

Counts are labelled honestly. A count backed by a source is shown as such; a number you chose for yourself is shown as your own goal and never dressed up as a recommendation. This is a counting tool, not a religious authority — if something here is wrong, we would rather hear it than have it stand.

Free, and What Is Paid

The counter and everything you need to remember Allah are free permanently. An optional one-time Premium plan pays for the running costs: it removes advertising and adds sync across your devices and daily reminders. Convenience, in other words — never the ability to count your dhikr.
