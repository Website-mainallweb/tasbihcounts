About Us

Our Spiritual Mission

Welcome to Bhakti Nam Jap Counter, your dedicated digital companion for mantra meditation and daily spiritual practice. Founded with deep respect for ancient traditions, Bhakti Nam Jap Counter connects timeless devotional practices with the ease of modern technology, helping you stay focused, peaceful, and spiritually grounded.

What We Offer

Bhakti Nam Jap Counter is a beautiful and intuitive online nam jap counter designed for spiritual seekers who wish to track their mantra repetitions, count mala rounds, and maintain daily discipline in their meditation.
You can chant the names of your chosen deities like Shri Ram, Shri Krishna, Hanuman, Shiva, Durga, or any custom mantra you prefer.
With Bhakti Nam Jap Counter, your sadhana becomes organized, peaceful, and deeply fulfilling.

Our Vision

At Bhakti Nam Jap Counter, we believe that technology should support spiritual growth, not interrupt it.
Our mission is to make chanting and meditation accessible to everyone—whether you are a beginner learning to focus your mind or an experienced devotee maintaining a lifelong spiritual habit.
Through this online nam jap counter, we aim to inspire mindfulness, devotion, and consistency in every practitioner’s life.

Features That Matter

Simple Counting – Easy-to-use digital counter for each mantra repetition.
Multiple Deities – Support for all major Hindu deities and custom mantras.
Progress Tracking – Visual progress bars that show your mala completion and daily goals.
Meditation Timer – Built-in timer to track your chanting sessions.
Privacy Focused – Your spiritual practice stays completely personal and secure.
Free Forever – The counter, streaks and stats are free for everyone, with no subscriptions. An optional one-time Premium adds sync across devices, reminders and an ad-free page.

The Bhakti Nam Jap counter is and will always remain free to use. Premium is optional and never needed to count, chant or keep your streak.
We are devoted to offering this tool as a seva—a service to the spiritual community around the world.
