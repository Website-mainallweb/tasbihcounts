What Is Bhakti Nam Jap?

Bhakti Nam Jap is a digital Nam Jap counter (नाम जप काउंटर) for chanting a divine name or mantra online. As you chant, it shows Today’s Nam Jap, your Total Nam Jap and your Total Mala Jap, so you always know where your practice stands. It brings the discipline of a physical mala to the phone or computer you already carry.

How to Use the Bhakti Nam Jap Counter

Starting takes a few seconds:

  • Choose the name of the deity you want to chant from the name list.
  • If your name or mantra is not in the list, add it as your own and chant it.
  • Tap the counter ring to count. Every tap counts one Nam Jap; on a computer you can also press Space.
  • Watch your Today, Total and Total Mala counts grow.

That is all it takes to begin your Nam Jap with the Bhakti Nam Jap Counter.

What Is a Mala in the Nam Jap Counter?

In the Bhakti Nam Jap Counter, a Mala is one complete round of 108 repetitions, just as a physical mala has 108 beads. After 108 counts — or the target you set — one Mala is complete, and the next round begins straight away, so your chanting never has to stop.

Understanding the Counter Stats

The counter shows three numbers: Today’s Jap, Total Jap and Total Mala Jap.

  • Today’s Jap: how many times you have chanted today.
  • Total Jap: every count you have ever made with the counter.
  • Total Mala: every mala you have completed.

The Streak and Stats pages go further, with your days in a row and your weeks, months and years of practice.

Using the Count and Reset Buttons

Two controls do most of the work:

  • Reset: Opens the reset options — undo the last count, set the counter back to zero, finish the session, clear today, or erase everything. Setting the counter back to zero keeps your Today and Total counts and your history.
